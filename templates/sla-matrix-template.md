# Response SLA Matrix Template

Copy and fill in. Speed-to-lead is a measured SLA, not a value statement.

| Lead type | First human touch | Owner | Touches in 14 days | Channels | Escalation if missed |
|-----------|------------------|-------|--------------------|---------|---------------------|
| Demo request | < 5 min (business hours) | SDR queue | 8-12 | Call, email, LinkedIn | Auto-reassign + manager alert at 15 min |
| Contact / hand-raiser | < 15 min | SDR queue | 8-10 | Call, email | Reassign at 1 hour |
| Trial signup (sales-assisted) | < 1 hour | SDR queue | 6-8 | Email, call on engagement | Reassign at 4 hours |
| Standard MQL | < 1 hour | SDR queue | 6-8 | Call + email + social | Reassign at 4 hours |
| Event follow-up | < 4 hours | SDR queue | 5-6 | Email-first | Manager review daily |
| Recycled / re-engaged | < 4 hours | SDR queue | 5-6 | Email-first, call on signal | Reassign at 1 business day |
| Off-hours (all types) | Next business day start | SDR queue | Per type above | Per type above | Report as off-hours; do not fake the timestamp |

## Rules

- The clock starts at routing, not at creation. Measure both; optimize routing delay separately.
- "First human touch" means a call, a personal email, or a real reply. An automated "thanks for downloading" email is not a touch.
- Every missed SLA reassigns automatically and notifies the manager. Manual reassignment is how SLAs die.
- Report median, not average. Averages hide the queue of shame.
- Off-hours leads get an instant automated acknowledgment with a named human and a time ("Priya will call you tomorrow at 9am CT"). Silence until morning is worse than a bot that sets expectations.
