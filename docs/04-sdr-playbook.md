# 04 - SDR Playbook

The operating system for the team that works the top of funnel. Tools and routing create the opportunity to sell; this is what good execution looks like.

## Working cadence

| Lead type | First touch | Touches in 14 days | Channels |
|-----------|------------|--------------------|---------|
| Demo request / hand-raiser | < 5 min | 8-12 | Call, email, LinkedIn, SMS if opted in |
| MQL | < 1 hour | 6-8 | Call + email + social |
| Recycled / re-engaged | < 4 hours | 5-6 | Email-first, call on engagement |

The first touch is a call whenever a phone number exists. Email-only follow-up on a demo request is leaving money on the table.

## Sequences, not blasts

A sequence is a timed, multi-channel touch pattern with a point of view. Principles:

- Every touch adds information or asks a real question. "Bumping this to the top of your inbox" is not a touch.
- Reference the signal: the page they visited, the event they attended, the tech they use.
- End sequences cleanly with a breakup touch that invites a future conversation, then recycle to nurture with context.

## Qualification

Use a framework the whole org agrees on. MEDDPICC-lite for complex deals, BANT for velocity:

- **Need / Pain:** What problem, what happens if unsolved, who feels it.
- **Authority:** Are we talking to the person who can say yes, or someone who can only say no?
- **Timeline:** Is there a real date driving this, or is it curiosity?
- **Fit:** Do they match the ICP closely enough to win and retain?

Document the answers on the lead before conversion. An SQL with empty qualification fields is an MQL with confidence.

## The AE handoff

The handoff is a meeting, not a field update:

1. SDR books the discovery call with the AE invited, on the prospect's calendar.
2. Handoff notes: pain, stakeholders mapped so far, timeline, what was promised.
3. AE accepts or rejects within 24 hours with a reason. Rejections go back to nurture with notes, not to limbo.
4. Track acceptance rate. Below ~70% means qualification criteria or scoring needs work, not that SDRs need "coaching."

## Metrics that matter

- MQL → SQL conversion (by source, by SDR)
- Speed-to-lead (median, not average)
- Touches per SQL (effort efficiency)
- AE acceptance rate (qualification quality)
- Pipeline sourced per SDR (outcome, not activity)

Activity metrics (calls made, emails sent) are diagnostic, not goals. Measure them to find coaching opportunities, not to set targets.
